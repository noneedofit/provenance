Summary

  • In short: ALEXPEDITION AS is an established company in "reisearrangørvirksomhet ellers" based in Asker, profitable in 2025.[1][2][3][4]
  • ALEXPEDITION AS is registered in the industry "Reisearrangørvirksomhet ellers"; its registered activity is "Turoperatør som arrangerer eventyrreiser, ekspedisjoner, og friluftsliv kurs i høyfjell og kalde strøk over hele verden.".[2][5]
  • ALEXPEDITION AS is a private limited company (AS), founded on 2018-11-10, registered in Asker.[6][1][3]
  • It reported revenue of NOK 117,697 for 2025 and an operating result of NOK 4,726 (2025).[7][8]
  • Its 2025 accounts show an operating margin of 4.0% and a moderate equity ratio of 36% of total assets.[7][8][9][10]
  • Revenue rose 129.8% from 2024 to 2025 (NOK 51,207 to NOK 117,697); it was NOK 82,882 in 2023. The annual result was a profit in 2025, as in 2024 (2023: -NOK 2,004, 2024: NOK 1,829, 2025: NOK 4,702).[11][12][7][13][14][4]
  • ALEXPEDITION AS is led by board chair Alexander Konrad Steurenthaler.[15]
  • The register last recorded a change to its roles on 2018-11-21.[16]
  • ALEXPEDITION AS has 1 registered workplace.[17]
