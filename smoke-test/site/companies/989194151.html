Summary

  • In short: TREKANTBYGGET EIENDOM AS is an established company in "utleie av egen eller leid fast eiendom" based in Bergen, profitable in 2025.[1][2][3][4]
  • TREKANTBYGGET EIENDOM AS is registered in the industry "Utleie av egen eller leid fast eiendom"; its registered activity is "Investere i fast eiendom eller selskaper som eier fast eiendom, og alt som dermed står i forbindelse.".[2][5]
  • TREKANTBYGGET EIENDOM AS is a private limited company (AS), founded on 2005-12-16, registered in Bergen.[6][1][3]
  • It reported revenue of NOK 2.49M for 2025 and an operating result of NOK 1.56M (2025).[7][8]
  • Its 2025 accounts show an operating margin of 62.7% and a moderate equity ratio of 28% of total assets.[7][8][9][10]
  • Revenue rose 8.5% from 2024 to 2025 (NOK 2.29M to NOK 2.49M); it was NOK 1.98M in 2023. The annual result was a profit in each of the 3 filed years read (2023: NOK 1.02M, 2024: NOK 862,860, 2025: NOK 891,480).[11][12][7][13][14][4]
  • TREKANTBYGGET EIENDOM AS is led by board chair Arild Faugstad.[15]
  • It has a board of 3. The register last recorded a change to its roles on 2026-10-06 (within the past year).[15][16][17][18]
  • TREKANTBYGGET EIENDOM AS has 1 registered workplace.[19]
