Summary

  • In short: FINNMARK MANUELLTERAPI AS is an established company in "aktiviteter innenfor tradisjonell, komplementær og alternativ medisin" based in Vardø, profitable in 2025.[1][2][3][4]
  • FINNMARK MANUELLTERAPI AS is registered in the industry "Aktiviteter innenfor tradisjonell, komplementær og alternativ medisin"; its registered activity is "Helsetjeneste, manuellterapi, fysioterapi, akupunktur, treningsveiledning, arbeidsplassvurdering/tilrettelegging.".[2][5]
  • FINNMARK MANUELLTERAPI AS is a private limited company (AS), founded on 2013-08-02, registered in Vardø.[6][1][3]
  • It reported revenue of NOK 3.89M for 2025 and an operating result of NOK 469,186 (2025).[7][8]
  • Its 2025 accounts show an operating margin of 12.1% and a thin equity ratio of 4% of total assets.[7][8][9][10]
  • Revenue rose 2.8% from 2024 to 2025 (NOK 3.78M to NOK 3.89M); it was NOK 3.47M in 2023. The annual result was a profit in each of the 3 filed years read (2023: NOK 424,974, 2024: NOK 197,575, 2025: NOK 309,974).[11][12][7][13][14][4]
  • FINNMARK MANUELLTERAPI AS is led by Terje Andreassen, who is both CEO and board chair.[15][16]
  • The register last recorded a change to its roles on 2019-06-05.[17]
  • FINNMARK MANUELLTERAPI AS has 1 registered workplace.[18]
