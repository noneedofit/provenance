Summary

  • In short: NAGRA AS is an established company in "konsulentvirksomhet tilknyttet informasjonsteknologi og forvaltning og drift av it-systemer" based in Kristiansand, loss-making in 2025 and with no open NAV job ads under its org number.[1][2][3][4]
  • NAGRA AS describes itself on its website as: "Practical guidance for leaders on governance, strategy, organisational change and programme management, with a growing focus on AI, cyber security and data protection. Assignments often begin as sh".[5]
  • NAGRA AS is a private limited company (AS), founded on 2021-03-03, registered in Kristiansand.[6][1][3]
  • It reported revenue of NOK 246,914 for 2025 and an operating result of NOK 6,542 (2025).[7][8]
  • Its 2025 accounts show an operating margin of 2.6% and a strong equity ratio of 56% of total assets.[7][8][9][10]
  • Revenue fell 65.6% from 2024 to 2025 (NOK 717,648 to NOK 246,914); it was NOK 906,731 in 2023. The annual result was a loss in 2025, as in 2024 (2023: NOK 96,949, 2024: -NOK 6,987, 2025: -NOK 1,335).[11][12][7][13][14][4]
  • NAGRA AS is led by board chair Abida Nagra.[15]
  • The register last recorded a change to its roles on 2021-03-12.[16]
  • NAGRA AS has a verified website (https://nagra.no/) and 1 registered workplace.[17][18]
