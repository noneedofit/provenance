Summary

  • In short: INNLANDET TAKSTSERVICE AS is a young company in "eiendomsforvaltning på oppdrag" based in Gjøvik, profitable in 2025.[1][2][3][4]
  • INNLANDET TAKSTSERVICE AS is registered in the industry "Eiendomsforvaltning på oppdrag"; its registered activity is "Takseringstjenester. Investering i andre selskaper. Eiendomsutvikling og utleie.".[2][5]
  • INNLANDET TAKSTSERVICE AS is a private limited company (AS), founded on 2022-07-08, registered in Gjøvik.[6][1][3]
  • It reported revenue of NOK 4.02M for 2025 and an operating result of NOK 1.97M (2025).[7][8]
  • Its 2025 accounts show an operating margin of 48.9% and a strong equity ratio of 43% of total assets.[7][8][9][10]
  • Revenue fell 3.6% from 2024 to 2025 (NOK 4.17M to NOK 4.02M); it was NOK 3.98M in 2023. The annual result was a profit in each of the 3 filed years read (2023: NOK 2.08M, 2024: NOK 1.33M, 2025: NOK 1.16M).[11][12][7][13][14][4]
  • INNLANDET TAKSTSERVICE AS is led by board chair Sindre Illøkken Eriksen.[15]
  • The register last recorded a change to its roles on 2022-08-03.[16]
  • INNLANDET TAKSTSERVICE AS has 1 registered workplace.[17]
