Summary

  • In short: IKOMM AS is an established medium-sized company in "datainfrastruktur, -behandling, -lagring og tilknyttede tjenester" based in Lillehammer, profitable in 2025 and with no open NAV job ads under its org number.[1][2][3][4][5]
  • IKOMM AS describes itself on its website as: "For kommuner, bedrifter og deg - Ikomm leverer sikker og stabil IT-drift for offentlige og private aktører. Vi sørger for at teknologien jobber for deg".[6]
  • IKOMM AS is a private limited company (AS), founded on 2007-05-14, registered in Lillehammer.[7][1][4]
  • It has 167 registered employees, reported revenue of NOK 493.33M for 2025 and an operating result of NOK 1.93M (2025).[2][8][9]
  • Its 2025 accounts show an operating margin of 0.4% and a moderate equity ratio of 22% of total assets.[8][9][10][11]
  • Revenue rose 15.0% from 2024 to 2025 (NOK 429.13M to NOK 493.33M); it was NOK 328.41M in 2023. The annual result went from a loss in 2024 to a profit in 2025 (2023: -NOK 10.17M, 2024: -NOK 5.90M, 2025: NOK 831,457).[12][13][8][14][15][5]
  • IKOMM AS is led by CEO Morten Kristoffer Rønning and board chair Vegar Strand.[16][17]
  • It has a board of 7. The register last recorded a change to its roles on 2026-07-13 (within the past year).[17][18][19][20][21][22][23][24]
  • IKOMM AS has a verified website (https://www.ikomm.no/), 2 linked public profiles and 2 registered workplaces.[25][26][27][28][29]
