Summary

  • In short: TANTE BRUUN AS is an established company in "interiørdesignvirksomhet" based in Oslo, loss-making in 2025.[1][2][3][4]
  • TANTE BRUUN AS is registered in the industry "Interiørdesignvirksomhet"; its registered activity is "Interiørdesign.".[2][5]
  • TANTE BRUUN AS is a private limited company (AS), founded on 2006-05-04, registered in Oslo.[6][1][3]
  • It reported revenue of NOK 571 for 2025 and an operating loss of NOK 390,215 (2025).[7][8]
  • Its 2025 accounts show an operating margin of -68338.9% and a moderate equity ratio of 21% of total assets.[7][8][9][10]
  • Revenue fell 99.2% from 2024 to 2025 (NOK 75,133 to NOK 571); it was NOK 3.71M in 2023. The annual result was a loss in each of the 3 filed years read (2023: -NOK 238,148, 2024: -NOK 125,651, 2025: -NOK 304,305).[11][12][7][13][14][4]
  • TANTE BRUUN AS is led by Camilla Synnøve Stene, who is both CEO and board chair.[15][16]
  • The register last recorded a change to its roles on 2025-09-02.[17]
  • TANTE BRUUN AS has 1 registered workplace.[18]
