Summary

  • TANTE BRUUN AS is registered in the industry "Interiørdesignvirksomhet"; its statutory purpose reads "Konsulenttjenester innen interiør og design, kjøp og salg av interiør og kunst, samt annet som naturlig står i forbindelse.".[1][2]
  • TANTE BRUUN AS is a private limited company (AS), founded on 2006-05-04, registered in Oslo.[3][4][5]
  • It reported revenue of NOK 571 for 2025 and an operating loss of NOK 390,215 (2025).[6][7]
  • TANTE BRUUN AS is led by CEO Camilla Synnøve Stene and board chair Camilla Synnøve Stene.[8][9]
