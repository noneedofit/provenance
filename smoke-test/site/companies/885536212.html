Summary

  • It reported revenue of NOK 17.81M for 2025 and an operating result of NOK 7.71M (2025).[6][7]
  • SLEMMESTADSENTERET AS is led by CEO Petter Christian Nissen-Lie and board chair Petter Christian Nissen-Lie.[8][9]
