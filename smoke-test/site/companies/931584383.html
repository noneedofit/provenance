Summary

  • HELLER ENTREPRENØR AS describes itself on its website as: "Heller Entreprenør AS – Byggefirma i Askim med over 40 års erfaring innen prosjektledelse, byggesøknader og konstruksjonsarbeid".[1]
  • HELLER ENTREPRENØR AS is a private limited company (AS), founded on 1982-01-01, registered in Indre Østfold.[2][3][4]
  • It has 19 registered employees, reported revenue of NOK 23.43M for 2025 and an operating loss of NOK 348,440 (2025).[5][6][7]
  • HELLER ENTREPRENØR AS is led by CEO Ole Christian Heller and board chair Steinar Ove Heller.[8][9]
