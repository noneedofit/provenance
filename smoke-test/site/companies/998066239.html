Summary

  • In short: PER O MEIER AS is an established micro company in "produksjon av tekstiler til teknisk og industriell bruk" based in Tønsberg, profitable in 2025 and with no open NAV job ads under its org number.[1][2][3][4][5]
  • PER O MEIER AS describes itself on its website as: "Per O Meier AS utfører vask, impregnering, spesialsøm og reparasjon av båtkalesje, kalesje, innvendig og utvendig solskjerming, markiser og båtputer samt presenning i Tønsberg, Sandefjord, Færder,".[6]
  • PER O MEIER AS is a private limited company (AS), founded on 2012-01-02, registered in Tønsberg.[7][1][4]
  • It has 7 registered employees, reported revenue of NOK 7.20M for 2025 and an operating result of NOK 1.13M (2025).[2][8][9]
  • Its 2025 accounts show an operating margin of 15.7% and a strong equity ratio of 68% of total assets.[8][9][10][11]
  • Revenue rose 13.7% from 2024 to 2025 (NOK 6.34M to NOK 7.20M); it was NOK 6.01M in 2023. The annual result was a profit in each of the 3 filed years read (2023: NOK 101,057, 2024: NOK 662,157, 2025: NOK 882,491).[12][13][8][14][15][5]
  • PER O MEIER AS is led by Ivan Johansen, who is both CEO and board chair.[16][17]
  • The register last recorded a change to its roles on 2026-05-05 (within the past year).[18]
  • PER O MEIER AS has a verified website (https://peromeier.no/), 2 linked public profiles and 1 registered workplace.[19][20][21][22]
