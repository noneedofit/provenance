Summary

  • In short: VILLA GABEL VBN AS is an established company in "pr og kommunikasjonstjenester" based in Oslo, profitable in 2025.[1][2][3][4]
  • VILLA GABEL VBN AS is registered in the industry "Pr og kommunikasjonstjenester"; its registered activity is "Markedsføringstjenester og næringslivsmesser.".[2][5]
  • VILLA GABEL VBN AS is a private limited company (AS), founded on 2013-01-23, registered in Oslo.[6][1][3]
  • It reported revenue of NOK 6.70M for 2025 and an operating result of NOK 104,080 (2025).[7][8]
  • Its 2025 accounts show an operating margin of 1.6% and a strong equity ratio of 46% of total assets.[7][8][9][10]
  • Revenue fell 1.1% from 2024 to 2025 (NOK 6.77M to NOK 6.70M); it was NOK 6.33M in 2023. The annual result was a profit in each of the 3 filed years read (2023: NOK 541,122, 2024: NOK 70,389, 2025: NOK 84,536).[11][12][7][13][14][4]
  • VILLA GABEL VBN AS is led by Jørn Ellingsen, who is both CEO and board chair.[15][16]
  • The register last recorded a change to its roles on 2022-05-05.[17]
  • VILLA GABEL VBN AS has 1 registered workplace.[18]
