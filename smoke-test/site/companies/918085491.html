Summary

  • In short: TPX EIENDOM AS is an established company in "utleie av egen eller leid fast eiendom" based in Fredrikstad, profitable in 2025.[1][2][3][4]
  • TPX EIENDOM AS is registered in the industry "Utleie av egen eller leid fast eiendom"; its registered activity is "Selv, gjennom hel- eller deleide selskaper eller ved samarbeid med andre virksomheter, å investere i eiendom.".[2][5]
  • TPX EIENDOM AS is a private limited company (AS), founded on 2016-09-23, registered in Fredrikstad.[6][1][3]
  • It reported revenue of NOK 72,000 for 2025 and an operating result of NOK 3,820 (2025).[7][8]
  • Its 2025 accounts show an operating margin of 5.3% and a moderate equity ratio of 15% of total assets.[7][8][9][10]
  • Revenue was flat from 2024 to 2025 (NOK 72,000 to NOK 72,000); it was NOK 72,000 in 2023. The annual result was a profit in 2025, as in 2024 (2023: -NOK 1,680, 2024: NOK 7,967, 2025: NOK 2,972).[11][12][7][13][14][4]
  • TPX EIENDOM AS is led by board chair Trond Paulsen.[15]
  • The register last recorded a change to its roles on 2018-06-15.[16]
  • TPX EIENDOM AS has 1 registered workplace.[17]
