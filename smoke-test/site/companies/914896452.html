Summary

  • ODDA RUTEBUSS AS describes itself on its website as: "Våre komfortable turbussar med klimaanlegg, kjøleskap og toalett utfører små og store transportoppdrag over heile landet".[1]
  • ODDA RUTEBUSS AS is a private limited company (AS), founded on 1979-12-12, registered in Ullensvang.[2][3][4]
  • It reported revenue of NOK 9.41M for 2025 and an operating result of NOK 382,583 (2025).[5][6]
  • ODDA RUTEBUSS AS is led by CEO Asbjørn Ystanes and board chair Asbjørn Ystanes.[7][8]
  • ODDA RUTEBUSS AS has a verified website (https://www.odda-rutebuss.no/) and 1 registered workplace.[9][10]
