  • In short: EGO NOR AS is a young company in "engroshandel med maskiner og utstyr ellers" based in Kongsvinger, loss-making in 2025.[1][2][3][4]
  • EGO NOR AS is registered in the industry "Engroshandel med maskiner og utstyr ellers"; its registered activity is "Import, salg og service av verktøy, utstyr og andre produkter/tjenester som naturlig hører til dette".[2][5]
  • EGO NOR AS is a private limited company (AS), founded on 2024-03-08, registered in Kongsvinger.[6][1][3]
  • It reported revenue of -NOK 36,344 for 2025 and an operating loss of NOK 145,145 (2025).[7][8]

  • EGO NOR AS is led by Trond Berkana, who is both CEO and board chair.[13][14]

  • EGO NOR AS has 1 registered workplace.[16]
