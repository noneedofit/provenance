Summary

  • In short: EGO NOR AS is a young company in "engroshandel med maskiner og utstyr ellers" based in Kongsvinger, loss-making in 2025.[1][2][3][4]
  • EGO NOR AS describes itself on its website as: "EGO POWER+ er 100 % dedikert til utvikling av batteridrevne maskiner til park- og hagebruk".[5]
  • EGO NOR AS is a private limited company (AS), founded on 2024-03-08, registered in Kongsvinger.[6][1][3]
  • It reported revenue of -NOK 36,344 for 2025 and an operating loss of NOK 145,145 (2025).[7][8]
  • Its 2025 accounts show negative equity of -NOK 216,944 (liabilities exceed assets).[9][10]
  • Revenue fell 142.4% from 2024 to 2025 (NOK 85,636 to -NOK 36,344). The annual result was a loss in each of the 2 filed years read (2024: -NOK 72,056, 2025: -NOK 189,104).[11][7][12][4]
  • EGO NOR AS is led by Trond Berkana, who is both CEO and board chair.[13][14]
  • The register last recorded a change to its roles on 2024-03-20.[15]
  • EGO NOR AS has a verified website (https://egonor.no/) and 1 registered workplace.[16][17]
