  • NORRØNA AS is a private limited company (AS), founded on 1966-03-30, registered in Kristiansand.[6][1][3]
  • It reported revenue of NOK 1.35M for 2025 and an operating loss of NOK 254,397 (2025).[7][8]
  • Its 2025 accounts show an operating margin of -18.8% and a strong equity ratio of 78% of total assets.[7][8][9][10]

  • NORRØNA AS is led by board chair Rune Myra.[15]
  • It has a board of 4. The register last recorded a change to its roles on 2026-04-17 (within the past year).[15][16][17][18][19]
