Summary

  • STIFTELSEN SOR describes itself on its website as: "Vi arbeider for gode levekår, økt kompetanse og styrkede menneskerettigheter for personer med utviklingshemming".[1]
  • STIFTELSEN SOR is a foundation (STI), founded on 2001-06-07, registered in Oslo.[2][3][4]
  • It reported revenue of NOK 10.52M for 2025 and an operating result of NOK 389,721 (2025).[5][6]
  • STIFTELSEN SOR is led by CEO Linn Løvlie Slette and board chair Karl Haakon Sævold.[7][8]
  • STIFTELSEN SOR has a verified website (https://stiftelsensor.no/) and 1 registered workplace.[9][10]
