Summary

  • In short: DINTANNLEGE AS is a young company in "tannlegetjenester" based in Ullensaker, profitable in 2025.[1][2][3][4]
  • DINTANNLEGE AS is registered in the industry "Tannlegetjenester"; its registered activity is "Tannlegetjenester.".[2][5]
  • DINTANNLEGE AS is a private limited company (AS), founded on 2021-12-22, registered in Ullensaker.[6][1][3]

  • DINTANNLEGE AS is led by Leon Nhat Tran Nguyen, who is both CEO and board chair.[16][17]
  • It has a board of 2. The register last recorded a change to its roles on 2026-01-19 (within the past year).[17][18][19]
