Summary

  • In short: DINTANNLEGE AS is a young company in "tannlegetjenester" based in Ullensaker, profitable in 2025.[1][2][3][4]

  • DINTANNLEGE AS is a private limited company (AS), founded on 2021-12-22, registered in Ullensaker.[6][1][3]
  • It had 3 full-time equivalents (2025 annual accounts), reported revenue of NOK 5.64M for 2025 and an operating result of NOK 757,232 (2025).[7][8][9]

  • DINTANNLEGE AS is led by Leon Nhat Tran Nguyen, who is both CEO and board chair.[17][18]
  • It has a board of 2. The register last recorded a change to its roles on 2026-01-19 (within the past year).[18][19][20]
