Summary

  • In short: INVEST SRG GRENLAND AS is an established company in "utleie av egen eller leid fast eiendom" based in Skien, profitable in 2025.[1][2][3][4]
  • INVEST SRG GRENLAND AS is registered in the industry "Utleie av egen eller leid fast eiendom"; its registered activity is "Kjøp og salg av fast eiendom og aksjer.".[2][5]
  • INVEST SRG GRENLAND AS is a private limited company (AS), founded on 1999-10-04, registered in Skien, filing its annual accounts as a parent company.[6][1][3][7]
  • It reported revenue of NOK 1.91M for 2025 and an operating result of NOK 1.04M (2025).[8][9]
  • Its 2025 accounts show an operating margin of 54.3% and a thin equity ratio of 9% of total assets.[8][9][10][11]
  • Revenue fell 9.5% from 2024 to 2025 (NOK 2.11M to NOK 1.91M); it was NOK 1.44M in 2023. The annual result was a profit in 2025, as in 2024 (2023: -NOK 125,390, 2024: NOK 535,153, 2025: NOK 263,372).[12][13][8][14][15][4]
  • INVEST SRG GRENLAND AS is led by CEO Roar Sivertsen and board chair Geir Helstedt.[16][17]
  • It has a board of 2. The register last recorded a change to its roles on 2025-03-31.[17][18][19]
