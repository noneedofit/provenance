Summary

  • In short: HALVOR EIENDOM AS is an established company in "utleie av egen eller leid fast eiendom" based in Lillestrøm, profitable in 2025.[1][2][3][4]
  • HALVOR EIENDOM AS is registered in the industry "Utleie av egen eller leid fast eiendom"; its registered activity is "Primært investeringer i fast eiendom.".[2][5]
  • HALVOR EIENDOM AS is a private limited company (AS), founded on 2013-03-14, registered in Lillestrøm.[6][1][3]
  • It reported revenue of NOK 288,650 for 2025 and an operating result of NOK 220,813 (2025).[7][8]
  • Its 2025 accounts show an operating margin of 76.5% and a strong equity ratio of 48% of total assets.[7][8][9][10]

  • HALVOR EIENDOM AS is led by Ole Jan Halvorsen, who is both CEO and board chair.[16][17]
  • The register last recorded a change to its roles on 2026-06-08 (within the past year).[18]
