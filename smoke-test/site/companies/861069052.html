Summary

  • In short: THOMSEN AGENTURER AS is an established company in "agenturhandel med møbler, husholdningsvarer og jernvarer" based in Bergen, profitable in 2025.[1][2][3][4]
  • THOMSEN AGENTURER AS is registered in the industry "Agenturhandel med møbler, husholdningsvarer og jernvarer"; its registered activity is "Agenturvirksomhet og konsulentvirksomhet, samt handel, markedsføring, deltakelse i andre sel- skaper med økonomisk virksomhet, og det som hermed står i forbindelse.".[2][5]
  • THOMSEN AGENTURER AS is a private limited company (AS), founded on 1991-06-07, registered in Bergen.[6][1][3]
  • It reported revenue of NOK 263,673 for 2025 and an operating result of NOK 49,460 (2025).[7][8]
  • Its 2025 accounts show an operating margin of 18.8% and a strong equity ratio of 97% of total assets.[7][8][9][10]
  • Revenue fell 3.2% from 2024 to 2025 (NOK 272,473 to NOK 263,673); it was NOK 318,450 in 2023. The annual result was a profit in each of the 3 filed years read (2023: NOK 77,258, 2024: NOK 42,659, 2025: NOK 37,553).[11][12][7][13][14][4]
  • THOMSEN AGENTURER AS is led by CEO Kjartan Keech Haaland and board chair Even Langeland Strand Thomsen.[15][16]
  • It has a board of 3. The register last recorded a change to its roles on 2026-04-14 (within the past year).[16][17][18][19]
  • THOMSEN AGENTURER AS has 1 registered workplace.[20]
