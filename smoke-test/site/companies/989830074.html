Summary

  • In short: MENGER INVEST AS is an established company in "kjøp og salg av egen fast eiendom" based in Oslo, profitable in 2025.[1][2][3][4]
  • MENGER INVEST AS is registered in the industry "Kjøp og salg av egen fast eiendom"; its registered activity is "Kjøp og salg av verdipapirer, eiendom og annet som måtte falle naturlig sammen med dette.".[2][5]
  • MENGER INVEST AS is a private limited company (AS), founded on 2006-02-28, registered in Oslo.[6][1][3]
  • It reported revenue of NOK 172,502 for 2025 and an operating result of NOK 146,340 (2025).[7][8]
  • Its 2025 accounts show an operating margin of 84.8% and a strong equity ratio of 100% of total assets.[7][8][9][10]
  • Revenue rose 1.2% from 2024 to 2025 (NOK 170,384 to NOK 172,502); it was NOK 190,749 in 2023. The annual result went from a loss in 2024 to a profit in 2025 (2023: NOK 26,787, 2024: -NOK 248,509, 2025: NOK 412,604).[11][12][7][13][14][4]
  • MENGER INVEST AS is led by board chair Morten Enger Grønvold.[15]
  • The register last recorded a change to its roles on 2022-08-04.[16]
  • MENGER INVEST AS has 1 registered workplace.[17]
