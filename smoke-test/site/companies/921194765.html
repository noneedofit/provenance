  • In short: OSLO SYD TRAFIKKSKOLE HOLMLIA AS is an established micro company in "undervisning ved godkjente trafikkskoler" based in Oslo, profitable in 2025 and with no open NAV job ads under its org number.[1][2][3][4][5]
