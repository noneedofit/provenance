Summary

  • In short: ALEAMD EIENDOM AS is a young company in "utvikling og salg av byggeprosjekter" based in Strand, profitable in 2025.[1][2][3][4]
  • ALEAMD EIENDOM AS is registered in the industry "Utvikling og salg av byggeprosjekter"; its registered activity is "Eie fast eiendom og utøve virksomhet i forbindelse med prosjektering og utvikling av fast eiendom, herunder kjøp, salg og utleie, og annen virksomhet i forbindelse derved.".[2][5]
  • ALEAMD EIENDOM AS is a private limited company (AS), founded on 2022-06-24, registered in Strand.[6][1][3]
  • It reported revenue of NOK 246,367 for 2025 and an operating result of NOK 144,547 (2025).[7][8]
  • Its 2025 accounts show an operating margin of 58.7% and a strong equity ratio of 100% of total assets.[7][8][9][10]
  • Revenue rose 459.4% from 2024 to 2025 (NOK 44,044 to NOK 246,367); it was NOK 1.64M in 2023. The annual result went from a loss in 2024 to a profit in 2025 (2023: NOK 76,983, 2024: -NOK 133,202, 2025: NOK 51,160).[11][12][7][13][14][4]
  • ALEAMD EIENDOM AS is led by board chair Aleksander Ingvaldstad Amdal.[15]
  • The register last recorded a change to its roles on 2022-07-23.[16]
  • ALEAMD EIENDOM AS has 1 registered workplace.[17]
